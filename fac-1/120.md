**Sublayer:** 2c Verification

**Goal.** The live verifier's two-run tests fail only when live-verify breaks its contract, never because the machine is busy. Today they time out now and then, "timed out waiting for both runs to reach Codex", and pass on a rerun.

Decided by the Human:
- 2026-11-05, on INN-25: kept in Triage, to be built after release 1 ([record](https://github.com/tuannd9288/factory/issues/120#issuecomment-6651162145)).

**Acceptance.**
- The cause is found and shown with evidence, such as timings of each step of a run under load, or a reproduction, before any fix.
- The tests do not fail under load: they pass many times beside several full verifies or a CPU burner.
- A broken contract still fails them: a throwaway change to live-verify that breaks it makes them fail, with each run saved.
- No wait is only made longer without the cause behind it.
- The SIGKILL test is checked against the same cause. It failed once on CI on 2026-11-05 and passed on retry ([record](https://github.com/tuannd9288/factory/issues/120#issuecomment-6655683071)).
- Seen so far: 2026-10-26 twice, 2026-10-27, 2026-11-01 and 2026-11-05.

**Limits.**
- Tier 1 if the change is in the tests only. A cause inside live-verify itself is a pack change.
- The tests never run real `claude` or `codex`, only their fakes.
