**Goal.** The quota table shows an account as used up when it is, wherever the limit was hit. Today it reads limit reports from seats' run logs and status-line files only. A limit hit inside a Lead's session is never read. On 2026-11-04 the table still showed an account at 94% a day after factory-probe's Lead had hit its weekly limit, and the seats' start hook repeated that figure.
