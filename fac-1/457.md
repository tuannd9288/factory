**Acceptance.**
- `factory use` alone prints one short line per team: the team and its account, with no prompt, numbers or letters.
- `factory use <account>`, run on either machine, moves the factory's team to that account: its row on the Mac mini, restarting its Lead, and on the MacBook the `--here` Lead and Colly, reloaded on it.
- With several teams, `factory use <team> <account>` names one, and `factory use <account>` alone refuses and names the teams.
- It says what moved in one or two plain sentences, and plainly what it could not move and why.
- An unknown account is refused, and the refusal lists the account names.
