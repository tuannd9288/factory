**Sublayer:** 0d Harness & session

**Goal.** Linear news the factory acts on reaches the right Lead within seconds, on the Mac where that Lead runs, and nothing is lost when the connection drops or the daemon restarts. Each Mac's daemon keeps a live connection to Linear and catches up on everything it missed each time it reconnects. This replaces polling and the MacBook relay for Linear news.

Decided by the Human:
- 2026-11-04, on INN-12: a live Linear connection on each Mac, built into the daemon in release 1, with a catch-up on every reconnect and no cloud mailbox ([record](https://github.com/tuannd9288/factory/issues/375#issuecomment-6626673502)).
- 2026-11-04: build it inside the existing design. His answer on an INN item wakes Colly through the factory's own events stream, and Colly's private listener goes once that works ([record](https://github.com/tuannd9288/factory/issues/414#issuecomment-6627722301)).
- 2026-11-04: the pick block on an INN item is in Vietnamese, so a tick is read by its option letter, never by its words ([record](https://github.com/tuannd9288/factory/issues/414#issuecomment-6630255479)).
- 2026-11-04: this issue takes over #399's measured day against piggery. His bar is that the daemon works better than piggery's ([record](https://github.com/tuannd9288/factory/issues/414#issuecomment-6633541158)).

- An option he picks later: no work on it until he does (2026-11-06, 15:00 +07, [record](https://github.com/tuannd9288/factory/issues/375#issuecomment-6680171489)).
