**Sublayer:** 0b Tools & permissions

**Goal.** A team on another harness, such as Codex, takes an issue from claim to an armed pull request by itself, with network, while it can read no credential but its own. In #133's two-team proof the Codex team ran offline and a Claude Code Lead claimed, pushed and opened the pull request for it, because Codex's sandbox could read every profile's token on the `factory` account. So that proof showed the claim works, not that a second team runs on its own.

Decided by the Human:
- 2026-10-31: a team on another harness comes in the next release, after several pstack teams run, each on its own account ([record](https://github.com/tuannd9288/factory/issues/131#issuecomment-6542056916)).

**Acceptance.**
- On the scratch repository, a Codex team, with no Claude session acting for it, claims an issue, commits the change, pushes its branch and opens a pull request that carries `Team: codex` and goes green.
- During that run, reads of another profile's token file, the Human's gh login and the agents' SSH key from inside the team's sandbox are refused, each shown in the run's log.
- The team's brief says what a refusal is: the sandbox or a permission rule, not a failing command. It also says whether a refusal of a command's form stops the work. On #133, Codex read the old line too widely and the Claude worker too narrowly ([record](https://github.com/tuannd9288/factory/issues/199#issuecomment-6516327589)).

**Limits.**
- The core stays as it is: the claim, the Gate block, verify and the required checks (#131).
- Which account the team uses is the Human's choice. Nothing here picks one.
- No credential is printed or copied into a log.
- The repository's deny rules bind Claude Code only, so what must hold for another harness holds on GitHub's side or in that harness's own sandbox.

**Prior art.**
- The factory's live verifier runs on its own Codex profile, which only the Human logs in.
- #134's credential probe: 16 of 16 reads refused for the seats.
