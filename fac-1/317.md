**Sublayer:** 0c Model configuration

**Goal.** The Human can let a team start on another signed-in account when its own cannot be used. Today a team whose account is signed out or removed cannot start a new session: it refuses and names the command to add that account, and the team waits for him. With a switch he turns on, a new session would start on another signed-in account instead.

Decided by the Human:
- 2026-11-01: after reading how agent-orchestrator handles this, he asked to record it or build it as a switch (the issue body).
- 2026-11-05, on INN-25: kept in Triage, to be built after release 1 ([record](https://github.com/tuannd9288/factory/issues/317#issuecomment-6651167123)).
- 2026-11-06: checked again after per-node accounts landed (#473). There is still no fallback, so it stays open ([record](https://github.com/tuannd9288/factory/issues/317#issuecomment-6680011955)).

**Acceptance.**
- With the switch off, the default, a team whose account cannot be used gets today's refusal, and nothing runs on another account.
- With it on, the team's next new session starts on another signed-in account, and the run's record says which account and why.
- A running session never changes account.
- Only he turns the switch on or off, with one command.
- Open: which accounts may be picked, any signed-in one or only those he lists, is his. Whether a moved session goes back to its own account at its next start, and where the switch lives, are the Lead's.

**Limits.**
- An option he picks later: no work on it until he does (2026-11-06, 15:00 +07, [record](https://github.com/tuannd9288/factory/issues/375#issuecomment-6680171489)).
- Off by default. Only he chooses accounts.
- Never touches a running session, and never logs in or moves a credential.
